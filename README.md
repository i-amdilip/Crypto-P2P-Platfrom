# 👑 RoyalMint – P2P Crypto Trading Platform

**RoyalMint** is a modern Peer-to-Peer (P2P) cryptocurrency trading platform designed to simplify digital asset trading by connecting buyers and sellers directly through an intuitive and user-friendly interface.

The platform focuses on seamless trading workflows, transparent transactions, real-time order tracking, and a professional crypto marketplace experience.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **P2P Trading Marketplace** – Connect crypto buyers and sellers directly.
- **User Dashboard** – Monitor account activity and trading statistics.
- **Buy & Sell Interface** – Explore available cryptocurrency trading offers.
- **Order Management** – Create, manage, and track trading orders.
- **Escrow-Based Workflow** – Designed around secure trade coordination.
- **Transaction History** – Keep track of previous trading activities.
- **Responsive Design** – Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Modern UI/UX** – Clean interface with intuitive navigation.
